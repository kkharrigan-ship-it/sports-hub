## Recheck list (`rights_unconfirmed: true`)

- curling-ca and curling-intl
- speed-skating and short-track
- gymnastics
- snowboard and freestyle
- biathlon

MotoGP's broadcast deal is confirmed. Only its free channel is uncertain, which shows in that Watch option's note rather than as a rights flag.

## Subscription verdict

- **ESPN+ / ESPN Select:** the only subscription worth considering, for the Grand Slam of Curling and the Rock League. Subscribe only in months with a Slam.
- **FloTrack, FOX One and HBO Max:** poor value year-round. At most one month each: the MotoGP finale, Le Mans.
